ZeroTrust

Secure & reliable messaging and file sharing.
Anywhere, anytime. No internet, no cloud, no server.


GitHub

What if you could collaborate with zero infrastructure? No Internet, no servers, no 3rd parties. ZeroTrust is a local, peer-to-peer, encrypted communication and collaboration platform that trusts no one, not even your own router. Whether you handle sensitive data, want better privacy, or simple don't have the infrastructure, our project can connect you to your peers, with no middleman. Already have a network? Our project can create a secure collaboration platform right on top of it, with no unwelcome guests. Heading somewhere remote? Any router or even your phone will do, regardless of Internet access. A natural fit for defence industry, emergency responders, air-gapped facilities, hospitals, critical-infrastructure operators and more.
